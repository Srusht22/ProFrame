# ProFrame — how to use it

For the person drawing the door or window. No technical knowledge assumed.

---

## Making a design

**1. Open the app.** You see your saved designs. Tap **New design**.

**2. Say what you are making.** Door or Window. Then PVC or Aluminium. Then a
colour — this is the colour of the door or window itself, not the app.

The profile system is chosen for you from the factory's settings. You can
change it, but you do not have to.

**3. Tap Start drawing.**

---

## Drawing

Draw the way you would on paper.

| To draw | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The outer frame | Draw a rough box. It does not need to be neat |
| A vertical divider | Draw a line down inside the frame |
| A horizontal divider | Draw a line across inside the frame |
| A sloping top | Draw the box with one side taller than the other |
| An opening panel | Draw a `<` or a `>` inside it. **The point marks the hinges** |

The app tidies the shakiness out of your lines. It does **not** tidy away a
slope you meant — if you drew one side taller, it stays taller.

If you draw something the app does not recognise, it is ignored. Your ink is
still kept, so nothing you drew is lost.

### The tools along the top

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Draw** | One finger draws |
| **Move** | One finger drags the sheet around |
| **Select** | One finger picks a panel or a divider, or drags a note label |
| **Fit** | Zooms so your drawing fills the screen |
| **Whole sheet** | Zooms back out |
| **Notes on / off** | Hides the note labels. It does not delete them |
| **Delete** | Removes whatever you have selected |

**Two fingers always pinch to zoom**, whichever tool you are using.

---

## Putting the sizes in

Tap any measurement on the drawing and type it. Sizes are in centimetres.

- **The number under the whole frame** is the total width.
- **The number beside it** is the total height.
- **The numbers under each panel** are the panel widths.

When you change one panel's width, **the panel next to it changes to match**,
so the total stays right. The app tells you which one moved.

If a width will not fit, the app says so and **changes nothing**. It will never
quietly give you a different number from the one you typed.

**A size in brackets** has not been confirmed — it was measured off your
drawing, not entered. Tap it and type the real one.

---

## Saying which panels open

**Long-press a panel.**

- **CH** means fixed. It does not open.
- **Z** means it opens.

If you choose Z, say how: hinged (and which side), tilt, or sliding. The app
states whether you are looking at the inside or the outside, because "hinges on
the left" means nothing without it.

In the same sheet you can also switch on **Mesh (توري)** or **Empty (فارغ)**.

---

## Notes

Two kinds, and they are separate.

**A note on the whole design** — the notepad icon at the top. General remarks,
what the customer asked for.

**A note inside one panel** — long-press the panel, then **Add a note**. Write
anything: توري, فارغ, "frosted glass", a reminder. You can add several to one
panel, edit them, hide them, or delete them.

**Moving a label** — switch to **Select**, then drag the label to wherever it
reads best. It stays inside its own panel, and it stays in the same place on
that panel if you change the sizes later. One drag is one **Undo**.

A note **describes** a panel. It never changes one. Writing "make this 2 metres
wide" does not resize anything — type the size in if you want that.

If you later split a panel in two, each note goes with the half it was written
on, and the app tells you it moved.

---

## Seeing it in 3D

Tap **3D Preview**.

- **Tap a Z panel** and it opens. Tap it again and it closes.
- **Tap a panel with a note** to read the note.
- **Drag** to move the view, **pinch** to zoom.
- **The slider at the bottom** raises and lowers the view angle.
- **The flip button** turns the product around to see the other side.
- **Fit the view** puts it back.

Fixed (CH) panels never move. That is the point of them.

**Back to edit** returns to your drawing. Nothing is lost, however many times
you go back and forth.

---

## Saving

Tap the **save icon**. A dot beside the project name means there are unsaved
changes.

Your work is also kept automatically as you draw, so if the app closes
unexpectedly it offers to bring it back next time.

If a save fails you are told, in a red bar. You are never left thinking
something is saved when it is not.

**Tap the project name** to rename it.

---

## Sending it on

Tap the **share icon**, and pick one:

| | What it is | Can it be edited again? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Design sheet (PDF)** | The drawing, sizes, CH/Z legend, every note | No |
| **Drawing (PNG)** | A picture of the front view | No |
| **Project file (.proframe)** | The design itself | **Yes** |

Only the project file can be opened and edited again. A PDF and a PNG are
pictures.

To open one someone sent you: **Open a project file** on the projects screen.

---

## Factory settings

The gear icon on the projects screen. Set once, and every new design starts
from them: material, colour, units, whether drawings are read from inside or
outside, whether sizes mean the frame or the wall opening, and the fitting gap.

Changing these does **not** change designs you have already saved.

---

## Two things to know

**The profiles are examples.** The frame sizes in this version are generic
preview figures, not your supplier's. Everything on screen and in the PDF says
so. Replace them with real catalogue data before anything is manufactured.

**This is a design tool, not a manufacturing instruction.** The drawings and
the 3D are accurate to what you entered. Fabrication needs validated profile
data and a factory check.
